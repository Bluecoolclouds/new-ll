---
name: Referral token rewards
description: User-approved 5% referral policy and its financial boundaries.
---

Inviters receive 5% of the tokens actually credited by their directly invited users' successful wallet top-ups, not 5% of money paid or model usage.

**Why:** The user requested “5% … получает в токенах от пополнения токенов его приглашённых”.

**How to apply:** Apply the same rule to all wallet payment methods. A reward is additional credit, not a deduction from the invited user's purchase. Failed/unpaid orders and referrals beyond the direct inviter do not earn this bonus.

New top-up rewards go directly to spendable wallet balance and count toward lifetime referral earnings, but must not also become separately transferable referral credit.

**Why:** Crediting both balances would let the legacy transfer mechanism pay the same reward twice. Direct credit also avoids an extra claim step in Stratus.

**How to apply:** Award only once with successful order settlement. Do not infer permission to recalculate old successful payments or existing rewards from a change to the referral percentage.
